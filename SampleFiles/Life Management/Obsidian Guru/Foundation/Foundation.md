# Foundation

## Mission

Establish a reliable workflow for building, packaging, and publishing Obsidian plugins, and gain early community adoption.

---

# Areas of Development

Foundation develops the Obsidian Guru across one milestone:

## Community recognized developer
Gain initial traction in the community by reaching a cumulative total of **1,000 downloads** across all published plugins.

---

# Completion Criteria

The Foundation stage is complete when the total download count across all of your published plugins reaches 1,000.
