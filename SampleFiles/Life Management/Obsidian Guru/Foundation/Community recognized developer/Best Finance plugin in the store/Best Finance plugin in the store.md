# Objective: Best Finance plugin in the store

## Purpose

To build and maintain the premier personal finance plugin for Obsidian, offering deep customization, seamless integrations, and exceptional user experience.

---

# Desired Outcome

A stable, intuitive, and highly functional personal finance plugin that users rely on as their primary finance companion in Obsidian.

---

# Why This Matters

Personal finance tracking is a core use-case for Obsidian users; offering a polished, templateable, and keyboard-friendly solution fills a major gap in the community.

---

# Completion Criteria

This objective is complete when key features like user templates, keyboard support, QoL improvements, and comprehensive connection/sign conventions are documented and implemented.
