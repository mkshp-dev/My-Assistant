# Community recognized developer

## Purpose

To establish a presence and demonstrate early adoption of published Obsidian plugins in the community. This represents the transition from basic development to a recognized plugin author.

---

# Transformation

This milestone represents the transition from:

> "I have published plugins with few active users."

to

> **"My plugins are officially recognized and widely adopted by the community."**

---

# Objectives

The capabilities are grouped into four core objectives:

## 1. Professional Developer Workflow
Establish a robust, automated developer pipeline to manage plugin development, testing, documentation, and releases efficiently.

## 2. Best Finance plugin in the store
To build and maintain the premier personal finance plugin for Obsidian, offering deep customization, seamless integrations, and exceptional user experience.

## 3. Gallery Plugin with 6 most used providers
Build a powerful media gallery plugin for Obsidian that integrates with the six most commonly used hosting/storage providers.

## 4. MOC plugin with all the flexibility
Develop a flexible and customizable Map of Content (MOC) generator plugin that allows users to seamlessly map and visualize their vault's structure.

---

# Completion Criteria

* **Total Downloads**: Reach a cumulative total of **1,000 downloads** across all published plugins.
